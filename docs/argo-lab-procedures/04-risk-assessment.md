# 4. Risk Assessment

Every audit finding marked AT RISK or UNANSWERED enters the risk register and
gets scored on a 5×5 probability/impact matrix. The register is reviewed at
the weekly [P] meeting; scores move, or the review writes down why they
didn't.

## 4.1 Scales

**Probability**

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Rare — no current indicator |
| 2 | Unlikely — possible, no warning signs yet |
| 3 | Possible — at least one warning sign observed |
| 4 | Likely — multiple signs, or it has happened on a comparable show |
| 5 | Almost certain — already happening, not yet costed |

**Impact** — anchor to money *and* schedule; use the higher of the two.
`[CALIBRATE: money bands to Argonautas budget scale]`

| Score | Budget impact | Schedule impact |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | < 0.5% of post budget | Absorbed by buffer |
| 2 | 0.5–2% | Eats buffer, dates hold |
| 3 | 2–5% | A milestone slips internally |
| 4 | 5–15% | Delivery date at risk |
| 5 | > 15% | Delivery/release date missed or deliverable rejected |

## 4.2 Matrix and thresholds

Score = probability × impact.

| P\I | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|-----|---|---|---|----|----|
| **5** | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| **4** | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| **3** | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| **2** | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| **1** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

| Band | Score | Rule |
|------|-------|------|
| Green | 1–6 | Monitor; re-score weekly |
| Yellow | 8–12 | Named owner + mitigation plan with a date |
| Red | 15–25 | Mitigation mandatory before the next gate; producer informed; if raised at G1/G2, the gate does not pass with an open red |

## 4.3 Process

1. Source: audit findings, department warning signs (chapter 3), dailies.
2. Log in the Editorial Risk Register (template 7.3): description, department,
   gate tag, P, I, score, owner, mitigation, review date.
3. Weekly: re-score, record movement. A risk that sat red for two weeks with
   no movement escalates to the producer automatically.
4. At each gate: no open red risks pass the gate unmitigated; yellows pass
   only with an owner and a dated plan.
5. At wrap: closed risks with real costs feed chapter 5 (budget impact) and,
   if instructive, chapter 6 (case studies).
