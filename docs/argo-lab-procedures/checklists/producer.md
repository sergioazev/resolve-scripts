# Producer — one-page checklist

**[G1] Decide before greenlight**
- [ ] Post schedule built from the work, not backward from an untested release date
- [ ] Festival/distributor deliverables defined — they drive the back half of the schedule
- [ ] Target platforms' technical specs read **before** camera/pipeline choices
- [ ] Release strategy clear enough to plan mastering (DCP / IMF / broadcast) and who makes them
- [ ] VFX shots counted — a number, not an impression; hero shots challenged
- [ ] Practical / production-design alternatives to VFX explored on the page
- [ ] Original score vs library decided and budgeted
- [ ] Rights budget realistic: music, archival, artwork, trademarks
- [ ] AI usage policy set: what's allowed, what's disclosed, what's prohibited
- [ ] Project frame rate chosen against delivery requirements

**[G2] Confirm locked before shooting**
- [ ] Green-Light Review answered in writing (audit §7) — all four questions
- [ ] No open red risks (score ≥15) passing the gate; yellows have owners and dates
- [ ] Archive budgeted as a deliverable, not an afterthought

**[P] During production**
- [ ] Weekly risk register received; reds escalated to you actually get decisions
- [ ] VFX drift past threshold requires your sign-off — refuse shadow shots

**[PL] At picture lock**
- [ ] Picture Lock Report signed — including contingency plan for scenes at risk
- [ ] Nothing in the cut that can't be licensed
