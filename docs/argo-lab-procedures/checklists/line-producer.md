# Line Producer — one-page checklist

**[G1] Feed the greenlight decisions**
- [ ] VFX count, ADR exposure, and rights budget priced from the audit, not from hope
- [ ] Mastering (IMF/DCP) and accessibility carried as budget lines with lead times

**[G2] Lock before shooting — you own this gate with the post supervisor**
- [ ] Editing, sound, VFX on aligned timelines; buffer exists
- [ ] Time after picture lock for music, mix, grade, QC — on the calendar
- [ ] Studio/financier review cycles scheduled
- [ ] All versions (theatrical, broadcast, international, airline) costed
- [ ] International deliverables spec'd: textless, M&E, stems, dubbing materials
- [ ] Accessibility scoped with vendor, cost, lead time (captions, SDH, AD)
- [ ] Camera test through the full pipeline budgeted and scheduled before day one
- [ ] DIT/data: backup policy, storage from shoot ratio, named data owner
- [ ] On-set VFX supervision budgeted for green screen / volume days
- [ ] Music clearances started; E&O and clearance procedures in place
- [ ] Security requirements (studio/TPN, NDAs, watermarking) costed
- [ ] Archive plan budgeted (medium, retention, owner)
- [ ] Editor attached early enough to affect the shot list

**[P] During production**
- [ ] Weekly: risk register reviewed; mitigations funded or consciously declined
- [ ] VFX drift and ADR forecast tracked against their budget lines

**[PL] At picture lock**
- [ ] Remaining schedule and budget re-verified against the actual lock date
